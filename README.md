# AfriMart-KollyBright-Sales-DashBoard
Interactive Excel Sales Performance Dashboard analyzing revenue, profit, and unit sales across countries, products, and years. The dashboard uses PivotTables, KPI cards, charts, slicers, and navigation links to provide interactive insights into sales trends, product performance, and top-performing products for data-driven decision-making.

### KPI Questions
1. What is the total revenue generated?
2. What is the total profit?
3. How many units were sold?
4. How does overall performance change across different years?
5. Which country generates the highest revenue?
6. Which products contribute the most to revenue?
7. How has revenue changed from 2024 to 2026?
8. Which year recorded the highest revenue?
9. Which product generates the highest profit?
10. Which products have the lowest profit contribution?
11. Which product has the highest number of units sold?
12. Which products have the lowest unit sales?
13. Is there a relationship between revenue and units sold?
14. What are the top 5 performing products?
15. Which products consistently perform well across revenue, profit, and unit sales?
16. How does performance change when filtering by country?
17. How does sales performance differ between 2024, 2025, and 2026?

### Processes Followed to Develop the Dashboard
  * Worked on the sales dataset on Excel.
  * Reviewed the available fields and identified the metrics required for analysis.
  * Checked the dataset for missing or inconsistent values.
  * Ensured numerical fields such as revenue, profit, and units sold were correctly formatted.
  * Prepared the data for PivotTable analysis.
  * Created PivotTables to summarize sales performance.
  * Used PivotTables as the foundation for the dashboard's charts and calculations.

  * Created KPI cards to monitor:

     * Total Revenue
     * Total Profit
     * Total Units Sold

   * Created different charts to analyze business performance:

     * Column Chart: Revenue by Country
     * Bar Chart: Profit by Product
     * Bar Chart: Units Sold by Product
     * Combo Chart: Revenue and Units Sold trends
     * Line Chart: Revenue trends for 2024, 2025, and 2026
     * Pie Chart: Top 5 Products

  * Added Country slicer to filter the dashboard by country.
  * Added Year slicer to compare performance across 2024, 2025, and 2026.
  * Enabled dynamic interaction between dashboard visuals.
  * Added a search icon linking users from the dashboard back to the PivotTables.
  * Added a data icon linking users from the dashboard back to the original dataset.
  * This created an easy navigation system between the dashboard, analysis, and source data.
  * Checked the accuracy of KPI calculations.
  * Compared dashboard results with PivotTable results.
  * Tested slicers, charts, and navigation links to ensure they functioned correctly.
  * Organized the dashboard into clear sections.
  * Applied consistent formatting to improve readability and user experience.
  * Ensured important KPIs and visualizations were easily accessible.

### Outcome

The Excel Sales Performance Dashboard transformed raw sales data into an interactive reporting tool. It enables users to quickly monitor revenue, profit, and unit sales while analyzing performance by country, product, and year. Slicers and navigation links also provide an efficient way to explore the underlying data and PivotTable analysis.

### Key Insights
1. Revenue Performance: The dashboard identifies the countries contributing the highest revenue and highlights differences in sales performance across markets.
2. Product Profitability: Profit varies across products, allowing management to identify products generating the greatest contribution to overall profitability.
3. Unit Sales: Analysis of units sold reveals the products with the strongest and weakest sales volumes.
4. Revenue and Unit Relationship: The combination chart makes it possible to compare revenue against units sold and identify periods where higher sales volume translates into increased revenue.
5. Yearly Performance: Revenue trends across 2024, 2025, and 2026 provide a clear view of changes in business performance over time.
6. Top Products: The Top 5 Products visualization highlights the products making the largest contribution to overall sales profit.
7. Interactive Analysis: Country and year slicers allow users to drill into specific markets and periods, making the dashboard useful for comparative analysis.

### Project Conclusion

The Excel Sales Performance Dashboard successfully converted raw sales data into an interactive and visually engaging business intelligence solution. By combining KPI cards, PivotTables, charts, slicers, and navigation links, the dashboard provides a comprehensive view of revenue, profit, and unit sales. It demonstrates practical skills in Excel data analysis, PivotTables, data visualization, dashboard design, and interactive reporting.
